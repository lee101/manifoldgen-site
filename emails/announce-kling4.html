Kling 4.0 is live

Text, image, reference and video edit up to 30 s

H3 Video   ·   Studio   ·   API

Kling 4.0 is now in the studio and the API. It makes 3 to 30 second clips at 720p or 1080p, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 21:9, and comes in four flavours:

  • Text to video: about 17 credits per second at 720p, 26 at 1080p.
  • Image to video: start from a still, optionally land on an end frame. Same rates.
  • Reference to video: up to 10 images and 5 videos as @Image1 / @Video1 in the prompt. Same rates, about 21 / 31 credits per second when you add a video reference.
  • Video edit: change a 3 to 30 second clip with a text instruction. About 21 credits per second at 720p, 31 at 1080p.

From the API, use service":"video_generate" with the model name and poll /api/video-jobs/{id}:

curl -X POST https://manifoldgen.com/api/service \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"service":"video_generate","model":"kling-4.0-text-to-video","prompt":"A detective on a rainy rooftop looks through a telescope","duration":5,"resolution":"720p"}'

Model names: kling-4.0-text-to-video, kling-4.0-image-to-video, kling-4.0-reference-to-video, kling-4.0-video-edit. Read the Kling 4.0 guide.
