# Plot 2 deltas (read with ../../conjurerssoul/plot.md)

**Prologue, nine years before.** Winter flood, Wickhollow mill-race. Tomas Calder drowns pulling his son Orin (9) out. Vesper Rane (12), whose own mother was a keeper of the Ring and taught her the words, kneels at the body in the dark and seats him, against the one rule. Sera Valek arrives too late. Vesper's throat is cut for the working (the scar), Orin's left eyebrow is split on the mill-race stones (the other scar). Tomas sits up. For a moment he is only Tomas.

**Nine years.** The village hides it. Tomas works the mill, never ages, never sleeps, smiles one second too long. The children grow up loving a father who is also the Guest's front door. Vesper carries it as a debt, Orin as a secret he is not allowed to look at.

**The present.** The Order (Silver, Mara, Aldren) finds the Ring because nine years of a seated man made it leak. Everything in `plot.md` Acts I-III follows. New emotional spine: the love between Orin and Vesper is built on the original sin. Every tender moment is also a confession.

**Trailer reveals** (more than trailer 1): the prologue, the nine years, Sera's verdict, Lazlo, Noll, Veyra, Mara's discovery, Aldren's logic, Thorn the mirror, the First Mouth and the keepers' secret, the "lid" choice, the door. It stops before the choice resolves.

**Romance rules.** Adults only (18 and 21). Tension, not titillation: a hand at a throat scar, forehead to forehead, jealousy, a collar grabbed, an interrupted kiss. The father's wrong smile is always the interruption.
