# Child locks (prologue only; grief and peril, nothing else)

## ORIN_9
```
Orin Calder at 9, small thin boy, dark brown hair too long and uncombed, grey-green eyes, a smooth unmarked left eyebrow (it splits later, in the flood), soot-inked palms, oversized patched wool coat the colour of wet bark.
```
## VESPER_12
```
Vesper Rane at 12, tall for her age, pale, black hair worn long and tangled, gold-brown eyes, a bare smooth unmarked throat (the scar is made later, in the seating), too-old black mourning wool for her dead mother, soot-stained fingers, a mouth that already knows the end of the sentence.
```
## TOMAS_PROLOGUE
```
Tomas Calder, late 30s, male, mill-built, thick through the chest and forearms, dark brown hair with no grey, the same grey-green eyes as his son, unshaven kind heavy face, patched mill clothes, iron-stained hands, an easy human blink and a smile that lasts exactly as long as it should.
```

Continuity gates: Orin's eyebrow is whole in every pre-drowning shot and split in every later shot; Vesper's throat is bare until the seating shot and scarred in every later one. Tomas's temples go iron only from the resurrection onward.
